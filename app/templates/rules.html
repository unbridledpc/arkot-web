Server Rules

  1. No cheating. Bots, macros, and client modifications that automate play get the account banned.
  2. No exploiting. Found a bug? Report it in the helpdesk — abusing it is a ban.
  3. No real-money trading of items, characters, or accounts.
  4. Keep names clean. Offensive or impersonating names get force-renamed or removed.
  5. PvP is part of the world; harassment is not. Killing is fine, targeted hate is not.
  6. One person per account. Sharing accounts is at your own risk — no restorations for shared accounts.

Staff decisions are final. Rules may evolve as the world grows.
